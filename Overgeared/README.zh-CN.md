# 专属锻造 · Personal Forge

RimWorld 1.6 / 1.7，需要 Harmony。支持简体中文与英文，跟随游戏语言。

建造名匠锻炉，打开定制界面。选择武器、部件、材料、外观和附魔，确认属性与用料后设置数量并委托制作。
设计可保存为图样；完整名称、刻字和背景故事可在武器档案中查看。
护手、握柄、配重与材料影响真实属性。烈焰、寒霜、雷霆、剧毒、震荡、汲血附魔消耗材料并提供伤害和粒子效果。
新生成的敌对近战类人有概率携带定制武器，可缴获使用。模组设置中可调整出现率和附魔比例，默认分别为 15% 和 25%。
亲手锻造玩法目前隐藏。可选搭配 baolei 的动画增强。

## English

Requires RimWorld 1.6 / 1.7 and Harmony. English and Simplified Chinese follow the game language.

Build a master forge and open customization. Choose weapon parts, materials, appearance and enchantment, check stats and costs, then set the quantity and submit an order.
Save designs as plans and view names, inscriptions and stories in the weapon archive.
Guards, grips, pommels and materials affect combat stats. Flame, Frost, Thunder, Venom, Concussion and Blood siphon enchantments cost materials and add damage and particles.
New hostile melee humanlikes can carry lootable custom weapons. Mod settings control their frequency and enchanted share, defaulting to 15% and 25%.
Manual forging is currently hidden. Optional companion: baolei's Animation Enhancement.
